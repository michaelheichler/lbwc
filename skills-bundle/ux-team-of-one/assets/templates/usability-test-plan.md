## Rapid Usability Test Script
<!-- Show the design; ask how they think it works. Have them explain what they're seeing and what they'd do to advance at each step. Don't lead them. -->
- "Take a look at this. What do you think this is / what's it for?"
- "How would you {{task_1}}? Show me what you'd click or touch."
- "What do you expect to happen next?"
- "Walk me through what you're seeing on this screen."

## Five-Second Test (optional, for information hierarchy)
<!-- Show a key screen for exactly 5 seconds, then remove it from view and ask from memory. Run on home/start screens AND random lower-level pages. -->
**Screen tested:** {{screen}}
- Show for 5 seconds, then hide.
- "What do you remember seeing?"
- "What did you think the purpose of this page/screen was?"
- "(If unfamiliar) What did you think the product was?"

**Pass check:**
- Did they notice the most important message/info? <!-- if not, hierarchy is off -->
- Did they correctly interpret the screen's purpose? <!-- if not, balance of messaging/affordances needs work -->
- Could they identify the type of product? <!-- if not, revisit navigation/branding/messaging -->
