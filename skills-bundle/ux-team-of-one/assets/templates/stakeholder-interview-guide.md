## Interview Questions
<!-- These should roll up to your learning objectives; reword per role as needed. Offer to keep it to 30 min; explain up front what you're trying to accomplish. -->

**Role & relationship to product**
- What's your role in the organization, and how does it relate to the product?

**Goals & success**
- What are your goals and top priorities in your role?
- How do you know if you're successful? How do you measure success?

**Customers**
- How does your work impact customers? Who do you consider your primary customers?
- Can you describe them — their expectations, priorities, and goals?
- Why do customers use our products? What prevents them from using them?
- What questions about users/customers do you not currently have answers to?

**Competition**
- Who are your competitors, and how well are we doing compared to them?
- What do we do well? What could be improved? What differentiates us?

**This project (if applicable)**
- What are your goals for this project? Your vision for how it improves the product?
- Do you see any risks or red flags?
