## The Rules (read aloud)
<!-- Everyone has ONE job: assume the most critical, judgmental perspective they can and view the designs through it. Pretend you're a grumpy, time-pressed user juggling four things — or a tough senior leader who must approve this. One issue per sticky note, placed near where it was spotted. -->

## Run the Clock (15-20 min)
<!-- Silent, or discuss as a group — either way, everyone writes LOTS of notes. If they hesitate, prompt with: -->
- When you look at each screen/step, do you understand its fundamental purpose?
- What jumps out at you? Is it what *should* jump out?
- Do you know what to do to advance to the next step?
- What questions do you have about the info and functionality shown?
- Does this feel like a reasonable number of steps?
- Does anything feel too complicated or cumbersome?
- Is there any language that doesn't make sense — instructions, button labels, anything?
